Do you install SIRA-compliant CCTV for residential towers?

Yes — cameras across lobbies, corridors, parking and perimeters, installed per SIRA requirements with control-room monitoring.

Yes — perimeter intrusion detection, gate automation with ANPR, villa alarms, burglary-resistant glazing and compound-wide CCTV.

Absolutely — villa CCTV, video intercom, smart locks, alarms and gate automation, professionally installed and rated for UAE conditions.

Can you provide internet for all our tenants?

Yes — bulk managed internet as a building amenity, plus heatmap-engineered Wi-Fi for all common areas.
