Is the nursery software compliant with UAE regulations?

Yes — admissions, attendance and billing workflows are built around KHDA and ADEK requirements, with data handling aligned to the Child Digital Safety Law and UAE hosting.

Can parents see daily updates and photos?

Yes — the parent app shares daily reports covering meals, naps and activities, plus photos and direct messaging with teachers, in Arabic and English.

Do you install SIRA-compliant CCTV for nurseries?

Yes — cameras across all SIRA-mandated zones including classrooms, installed by an approved contractor with as-built drawings and 24/7 health monitoring.

Can you wire the network during our fit-out?

Yes — that's the best time. We run structured cabling, place Wi-Fi access points from a heatmap survey and build the comms room before the ceilings close.

Do you provide AMC for nurseries?

Yes — one AMC covers classroom tech, network, CCTV, intercom and software support across all your branches.
