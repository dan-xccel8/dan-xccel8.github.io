Questions, answered

Will our tills keep working if the internet goes down?

Yes — offline-first POS keeps billing through internet outages and syncs automatically when the connection returns.

Can you help reduce wastage on fresh items?

Yes — FEFO expiry rotation, near-expiry dynamic markdowns pushed to shelf labels, and wastage tracking by department.

Do you install electronic shelf labels?

Yes — ESL across all your stores with central price management, so shelf and till prices always match.

Can you connect us to Talabat and other quick-commerce apps?

Yes — integrations with Talabat, Noon Minutes, Careem and your own online ordering, with live stock sync.

Are you ready for UAE e-invoicing?

Yes — Peppol/ASP-integrated, VAT-compliant billing ahead of the deadlines, with multi-year record retention.

Do you provide AMC support for supermarkets?

Yes — one AMC covers all your stores: network, tills, devices, software and security, with fast response times.
