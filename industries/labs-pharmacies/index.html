FAQ

Questions, answered

Can your LIS connect to our analyzers?

Yes — bi-directional interfacing with major hematology, chemistry and immunoassay analyzers: orders go to the machine, results flow back automatically with delta checks and critical-value alerts.

Do lab results sync with NABIDH and Riayati?

Yes — our lab systems push results to the government health information platforms where required, with UAE data residency throughout.

Will the pharmacy POS work during internet outages?

Yes — the POS is offline-first: billing, dispensing and stock deduction continue without internet, and everything syncs automatically when you're back online.

Can you monitor fridge temperatures for cold-chain medicines?

Yes — wireless temperature sensors with 24/7 monitoring and instant alerts if a vaccine or insulin fridge goes out of range.

Do you provide AMC for labs and pharmacies?

Yes — one AMC covers analyzer IT interfaces, POS terminals, network, CCTV and backups across all your branches.
