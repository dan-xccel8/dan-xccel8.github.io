FAQ

Questions, answered

Our ERP can't handle dye lots and shade tracking. Can you fix that?

Yes — we implement textile-aware inventory with roll-by-meter stock, dye-lot and shade-family genealogy, so shade issues stop causing costing errors and stock mismatches.

Can you put RFID tags on our garments?

Yes — RFID tagging on garments is standard in this trade. We supply the tags, readers and software for near-instant stock counts and automatic dock-door manifests.

Our buyers order on WhatsApp. Do we have to change that?

No — we build your B2B catalog and ordering to plug into WhatsApp: order capture, price-list broadcasts and shade-accurate photos, right inside the chat.

Are you ready for the UAE e-invoicing mandate?

Yes — we set up Peppol/ASP-integrated, VAT-compliant billing ahead of the July 2026 deadline, with 5-year record retention.

Do you provide AMC support for textile wholesalers?

Yes — one AMC covers your whole operation: network, devices, software and security, with fast response times.
