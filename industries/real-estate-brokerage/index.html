FAQ

Questions, answered

Can you integrate our CRM with Dubizzle, PropertyFinder and Bayut?

Yes — listings publish to all three portals and more from one place, and every portal enquiry flows back into the CRM automatically.

How do you distribute leads between agents?

Rule-based assignment — round-robin, area or expertise — with duplicate detection, leakage alerts and full visibility for managers.

Do you handle Trakheesi permit tracking?

Yes — every advert carries its permit field before publishing, keeping your listings compliant.

Can you connect our branches?

Yes — VPN and SD-WAN between offices, with a centralized CRM and centralized call handling across all branches.

How do you protect our client data?

Endpoint protection on every device, email anti-phishing, DLP on client and transaction data, role-based access and full audit trails.
