Ransomware in the UAE: A Practical Defense Checklist for SMEs

Ransomware attacks in the region keep rising. Here's the layered defense we deploy for small and mid-size UAE businesses — firewall to backup.

Ransomware doesn’t only hit enterprises. In the UAE, SMEs are frequent targets precisely because their defenses are thinner. The good news: a layered approach with the right tools stops the vast majority of attacks.

The checklist

Perimeter: A next-generation firewall (Fortinet, SonicWall or Barracuda) with intrusion prevention and DNS filtering — your first and most important gate.

Endpoints: Business-grade EDR on every laptop and server. Bitdefender, Sophos or Trend Micro catch what slips past the firewall.

Email: Most ransomware arrives by email. Microsoft 365’s built-in Defender plus user awareness training closes the biggest entry point.

Backup — the non-negotiable: Assume breach. Immutable, off-site backups (Veeam, Acronis or ExaGrid) mean you can restore instead of paying. Test restores quarterly.

Patching: Unpatched VPNs and servers are the second most common entry point. Automate it.
