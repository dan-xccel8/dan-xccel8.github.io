Zoho One vs Microsoft 365: Which Fits Your UAE Business?

X XCCEL8 Team
3 October 2026·2 min read

As a Zoho Authorised Partner who also deploys Microsoft 365, we get asked this constantly. Here’s our honest breakdown.

Choose Zoho One when…

  • You want one subscription covering 45+ apps — CRM, Books (VAT-ready for the UAE), Inventory, People, Projects and more.
  • Cost matters — Zoho One is typically a fraction of equivalent Microsoft licensing per user.
  • You need deep customization — Zoho Creator and Deluge scripting adapt the system to your processes, not the other way round.

Choose Microsoft 365 when…

  • Your team lives in Outlook, Excel and Teams — familiarity is worth real money.
  • You need advanced compliance and security tooling (Defender, Purview) out of the box.
  • You’re integrating with Dynamics 365 or Power Platform.

The third option: both

Many UAE businesses run Microsoft 365 for productivity and Zoho for CRM and finance — they integrate well. We implement either, or both, and support them under one AMC.
