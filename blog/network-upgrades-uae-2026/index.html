5 Network Upgrades Every Growing UAE Business Should Make in 2026

X Xccel8 Team
1 October 2026·2 min read

Slow Wi-Fi, dead zones in the warehouse, and a switch closet held together by hope — sound familiar? As UAE businesses grow, the network they started with stops keeping up. Here are five upgrades we recommend most often to our clients in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

1. Move to Wi-Fi 6E or Wi-Fi 7 access points

Office density keeps rising. Modern access points from Ruckus, Extreme Networks or NETGEAR handle hundreds of concurrent clients without breaking a sweat — and the 6 GHz band in Wi-Fi 6E gives you clean spectrum in crowded business districts.

2. Audit your structured cabling

Most “mystery” network problems trace back to cabling. A Nexans-certified Cat6A backbone future-proofs your office for 10 Gbps and PoE++ for cameras, access points and phones.

3. Segment with managed switching

Guest Wi-Fi, IoT devices, CCTV and staff laptops should never share one flat network. Managed switches let you isolate traffic with VLANs — a basic security step many SMEs skip.

4. Add UPS-backed power to the rack

A 10-minute power blip shouldn’t take down your phones and internet. APC or Vertiv UPS units sized to your rack keep you running through DEWA fluctuations.

5. Plan for the branch office now

If expansion is on the roadmap, design the network once: SD-WAN or site-to-site VPN between branches, centrally managed from day one.
