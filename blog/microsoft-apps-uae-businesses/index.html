Every Microsoft app, one expert team: implementing Microsoft for UAE businesses

X XCCEL8 Team
21 November 2026·6 min read

Most UAE businesses already own more Microsoft than they use. The licences are paid for, the products are world class in their categories — and then they sit half-configured, because each one was set up at a different time, by a different person, with nobody responsible for how they work together. The products are not the problem. Implementation is. This is the case for treating Microsoft as one platform — ten products, one expert team — and it is why we built our Microsoft practice around implementing the whole stack for UAE businesses, not selling licences and walking away.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft’s ten business products are each world class in their category — the value multiplies when they are implemented as one connected platform.
  • The common failure is not the software; it is piecemeal setup — different settings, different admins, no single design.
  • One implementation team across all ten means identity, security and data are designed once, properly, instead of patched together.
  • Xccel8 implements and supports the full Microsoft stack for UAE businesses — licensing, setup, migration, security and ongoing management.

Why do the ten products work better together?

Because they share one identity, one security model and one admin approach. The same sign-in that opens a user’s email also governs their files, their business applications and their device. A security alert in one product can trigger protection in another. Set up separately, each product still works — but the connections between them, which is where most of the value and most of the risk live, are left to chance. Set up as a platform, they reinforce each other.

The ten products — and why each is world class in its category

Dynamics 365 — CRM and ERP in one family: sales, service, finance and operations on the same data. It is world class because the business application and the productivity tools your team already uses are the same platform — a quote, an invoice and the email thread about it live together.

Microsoft 365 — the productivity standard: Teams, Outlook, Word, Excel and SharePoint. It is world class because it is the environment your staff already know, continuously developed, with collaboration and file storage built in rather than bolted on.

Microsoft Azure — cloud infrastructure that scales from one virtual server to a full platform. It is world class for its breadth — compute, storage, databases, AI services and security tooling under one roof, with data-residency options that suit UAE businesses.

Microsoft Entra ID — identity and access: single sign-on and multi-factor authentication for every app your team touches. It is world class because identity is the front door to everything else, and Entra guards that door for the whole estate at once.

Microsoft Intune — device management for every laptop, phone and tablet, in the office or at home. It is world class because it manages the device and protects the company data on it — including staff-owned phones — from one console.

Microsoft Defender Suite — endpoint, email and identity threat protection. It is world class because the protection is native to the platform — the email filter, the device agent and the identity monitoring see the same attacks and share what they learn.

Microsoft Purview — data governance and compliance: know what sensitive data you hold, where it is, and who touches it. It is world class because compliance stops being a spreadsheet exercise and becomes something the platform enforces and evidences.

Microsoft Sentinel — cloud SIEM: security logs from everything, correlated in one place, with threats surfaced fast. It is world class because it watches the whole estate — Microsoft and beyond — and turns millions of events into a short list of incidents worth acting on.

Microsoft Defender for Cloud — security posture management across Azure and hybrid cloud. It is world class because it continuously grades your cloud configuration, flags the weaknesses attackers actually exploit, and tells you what to fix first.

Microsoft Priva — privacy management: subject-rights requests and personal-data risk, handled systematically. It is world class because privacy obligations keep growing, and Priva turns them from a manual scramble into a managed process.

Why does the implementation partner matter more than the licences?

Anyone can sell you the licences — they are the same licences whoever you buy them from. What differs is what happens next: whether your identity is configured with proper multi-factor and conditional access, whether your data is classified before it leaks rather than after, whether the security products are tuned to your business or left on defaults that either miss attacks or drown your team in alerts. The products are world class out of the box; the results only become world class when the implementation is.

Why are we the best-placed team for Microsoft in the UAE?

Because we implement the whole stack, as one team, from here. Most providers cover a slice — the licences, or the migration, or the security piece — and the gaps between slices become your problem. We cover all ten products under one roof, we work in your timezone and your working week, and the same team that designs your setup is the team that supports it afterwards. For a UAE business, that combination — full-stack Microsoft expertise with local accountability — is exactly what “best placed” should mean.

Frequently Asked Questions

We already have Microsoft licences. Can you still help?

Yes — that is the most common starting point. We review how your existing products are configured, close the gaps (identity, security settings, unused products you are paying for), and connect the pieces properly. No rip-and-replace.

Do we need all ten products?

No. Most businesses need a core — Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune and Defender — and add others as they grow or as compliance requires. We will tell you honestly which ones earn their place for your business and which can wait.

Can you migrate us from Google Workspace or another platform?

Yes. Email, files and identities can be migrated with a planned cutover that keeps your team working throughout — and if a hybrid setup suits you better, we will say so.

Who supports us after implementation?

We do — the same team that set it up. Support from people who already know your configuration is faster and safer than a ticket queue that starts from zero every time.

How do we start?

With a review of what you have: licences, configuration and the gaps between them. You get a plain-language picture of where you stand and what we would do first — before you commit to anything.
