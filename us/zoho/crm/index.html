FAQ

CRM questions, answered

+
How much does Zoho CRM cost in the US?

Per user per month, billed annually: Standard $14, Professional $23, Enterprise $40, Ultimate $52 — or $20 / $35 / $50 / $65 billed monthly. The Free edition covers up to 3 users and never expires. Prices are before tax; US sales tax is added where it applies. Licenses are billed by Zoho directly.

Where is our CRM data stored?

In the United States. Accounts created on zoho.com are hosted in Zoho's US region — Quincy, Washington as the primary data center and Dallas, Texas as the secondary. The region is chosen when the account is created and cannot simply be switched later, which is why we confirm it with you before setup begins.

Is Zoho CRM HIPAA compliant — and will Zoho sign a BAA?

Zoho CRM supports HIPAA requirements and is covered by Zoho's SOC 2 and HIPAA audits, and Zoho will sign a Business Associate Agreement on request. It is not compliant by default: no patient data should go into the system before the BAA is signed, and ePHI fields must be marked for encryption with role-based access and MFA enforced. That configuration is part of our implementation for healthcare clients.

Does Zoho CRM meet CCPA and state privacy laws?

Zoho acts as a service provider for your customer data and states that it does not sell personal information. It supports the CCPA rights your customers hold — to know, delete and opt out — under its published California privacy policy. Your business remains responsible for honoring requests, and the CRM's export and deletion tools are how we set that up.

Can you migrate us from Salesforce or HubSpot?

Yes. Migration is a core part of our US implementations: we cleanse and map your data, rebuild your pipelines and automations in Zoho CRM, import your history, and validate the result with your team before go-live. Typical SMB rollouts, including migration and training, run 3–6 weeks.

Does Zoho CRM connect to QuickBooks, Stripe and RingCentral?

RingCentral, yes — Zoho's own free extension gives you click-to-dial, incoming-call pop-ups and automatic call logging. QuickBooks and Stripe connect through established Zoho Marketplace extensions that sync customers, invoices and payments; they are third-party extensions rather than built-in features, and we configure and test them as part of your setup.

What support do we get after go-live?

Zoho includes Classic support with 24×5 live chat on paid plans. On top of that, your training, changes and troubleshooting are handled by the team that built your system — people who know your setup.

How much does Zoho CRM implementation cost?

It depends on team size, data migration and integrations. We scope your process first and quote a fixed price before work begins — no open-ended hourly billing. A standard sales-team setup is at the lower end; multi-department rollouts with migration and several integrations are quoted individually after a free discovery call.
