Features

What Zoho Books does for your business

Invoicing & payment links

Invoices carry a pay-now link: Stripe, PayPal and ACH connected, links generated and shared from inside Books, with automatic reminders keeping payments on schedule.

Sales tax, calculated by address

There is no federal VAT here — instead, 45 states plus DC levy their own sales tax. Books' sales tax automation works out the right rate from the customer's address on every transaction, at no extra cost.

Nexus in plain sight

Selling into more states? Liability reports by state show an economic-nexus threshold approaching before you cross it, exemption certificates are stored on the customer record, and Avalara AvaTax connects when you need filing depth.

1099s without the January panic

Contractors are flagged as 1099 vendors with their W-9 details at setup, every payment counts toward their total, and 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC forms are e-filed from Books on the Standard plan and above.

Payroll across all 50 states

Zoho Payroll's US edition calculates, pays and e-files federal, state and local payroll taxes, runs direct deposit and issues W-2s — and posts each pay run's journals into Books automatically.

Dollars at home, every currency abroad

Your books run in USD; multi-currency invoices and bills convert at live exchange rates, so cross-border trade needs no shadow spreadsheets.

One design on every document

Custom templates carry one unified design across quotations, sales orders, invoices, retainers, credit notes, debit notes, purchase orders, vendor credits and payment receipts — your brand, identical on every document a customer or supplier sees.

Roles, approvals & access control

Custom roles decide who sees and does what, down to the module. Multi-stage approvals hold invoices, bills and purchase orders until the right people have signed off — nothing leaves unchecked.

Reports: standard & custom

50+ standard reports — profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow, sales tax liability by state — plus custom reports built around your questions, scheduled to the right inboxes automatically.

Inventory, your way

Start with Books' built-in inventory: stock levels, reorder alerts, price lists. Need warehouses, batches and serial numbers? We configure Zoho Inventory — or Books Elite — and connect the two properly.

Retail-ready with Zoho POS

Zoho POS is available in the US: counters bill fast, stock stays in sync with Books, and every location reports into one set of accounts. Already have a POS? We integrate it professionally instead.

Honest pricing in dollars

Zoho bills licenses directly in USD at the US list price — Free plan under $50,000 revenue; Standard $15, Professional $40, Premium $60 per organization/month billed annually. No markup; we charge for implementation, migration and training.

FAQ

Books questions, answered

+
How does Zoho Books handle US sales tax?

Sales Tax Automation calculates the correct rate from the customer's address on every transaction, using the states where you're registered — included on paid plans at no extra cost. We map the taxability of your products and customers, store exemption certificates, and set up liability reporting by state; if you sell into many states, we connect Avalara AvaTax for filing and nexus monitoring.

Is there an e-invoicing mandate in the US?

No — there is no federal e-invoicing mandate, and no federal VAT. E-invoicing here is voluntary (the Peppol network operates through the DBNAlliance, and some large buyers and government suppliers already exchange invoices this way). The compliance that actually bites for US businesses is sales tax and 1099 reporting — both are built into how we set Books up.

What does Zoho Books cost in the US?

Zoho bills licenses directly in USD: Free plan for revenue under $50,000; Standard $15, Professional $40, Premium $60 per organization/month billed annually. We recommend the plan that fits your actual usage — never seats or modules you don't need.

Can you migrate us from QuickBooks?

Yes — from QuickBooks Online and Desktop. We migrate your chart of accounts, customers, vendors, opening balances and transaction history, run the old system in parallel until the numbers agree, then switch over. A standard implementation typically takes 1–3 weeks; complex or multi-entity setups 4–8 weeks.

Does Zoho Books e-file 1099s?

Yes. Vendors are flagged as 1099 contractors with their W-9 details when they're set up, every payment through the year counts toward their totals, and 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC forms are e-filed from inside Books on the Standard plan and above — so January 31 is a report, not a project.

What about payroll?

Zoho Payroll's US edition runs payroll across all 50 states: federal, state and local taxes calculated, paid and e-filed; direct deposit; Forms 940, 941 and 944; W-2s through the employee portal. We connect it to Books so every pay run's journals post automatically.

Where is our data hosted?

US accounts are hosted in Zoho's own US data centers, in Quincy, Washington and Dallas, Texas, and the region is fixed at account creation. Zoho carries SOC 2 Type II certification, and we configure roles and access controls with CCPA expectations in mind.

Zoho Books vs QuickBooks Online — what do we gain by switching?

One connected suite instead of a patchwork: CRM, payroll, inventory and POS from the same vendor on one login, sales tax automation included rather than tiered away, and per-organization pricing that stays flat as your team grows. We migrate your QuickBooks history across cleanly, so nothing is lost in the move.
