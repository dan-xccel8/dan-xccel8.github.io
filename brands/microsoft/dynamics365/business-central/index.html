The Short Answer

What is Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central?

It is the ERP at the heart of Microsoft Dynamics 365, built for companies that have outgrown accounting software, spreadsheets and disconnected stock systems.

Everything shares one database. When a salesperson confirms an order, stock is reserved, the delivery is planned, the receivable is raised and the general ledger moves — at the same time, with no re-keying between systems. Finance sees what sales promised; the warehouse sees what purchasing ordered; management sees all of it, live.

Business Central runs in the cloud on Microsoft Azure, inside the same Microsoft ecosystem as Outlook, Excel and Teams, so your team works in tools it already knows. You subscribe per user, and Microsoft’s updates arrive automatically — there is no server to buy, patch or replace.

The Modules

What does Business Central actually run?

Thirteen modules, one database. Here is what each one does in plain terms — grouped the way your business is organised, not the way software menus are.

Group 1

Money & customers

The ledger, the pipeline and the buying desk. If your accounts live in one tool and your sales in another, this group is where they meet — and it connects directly to our Accounting & Finance and CRM & Sales services.

Financial Management

  • General ledger, payables and receivables in real time
  • Bank reconciliation with Copilot matching assistance
  • Fixed assets, budgets and cash-flow forecasts
  • Multi-currency, intercompany postings and consolidation
  • Dimensions — report by branch, project or business line

Sales & Customer Relationships

  • Quotes, orders and invoices in one unbroken flow
  • Built-in CRM: contacts, interactions, campaigns, opportunities
  • Customer pricing, discounts and price lists
  • Order promising, returns and drop shipments
  • Raise quotes and invoices without leaving Outlook

Purchasing

  • Requisitions and purchase orders with approvals
  • Vendor pricing, discounts and lead times on record
  • Three-way matching: order, receipt and invoice
  • Replenishment suggestions from live sales and stock data
  • Purchase returns and vendor performance history
Group 2

Stock, warehouse & the factory floor

From the shelf to the production line. This is the operational core for trading and manufacturing companies — the same ground covered by our Inventory & Logistics and Manufacturing & Supply Chain services.

Inventory Management

  • Live stock by location, bin and item variant
  • FIFO, LIFO, average, standard and specific costing
  • Lot and serial-number tracking, end to end
  • Stock-keeping units per location, cycle counts
  • Transfers between warehouses and branches

Warehouse Management

  • Receipts, put-away, picks and shipments
  • Bin structures from simple shelves to zoned layouts
  • Directed put-away and pick for busy warehouses
  • Barcode scanning on handhelds and phones
  • Internal movements and cross-docking

Assembly In Essentials

  • Assembly BOMs and kitting
  • Assemble-to-order straight from a sales line
  • Component availability checked before you promise
  • Assembly costs rolled into the finished item

Manufacturing Premium

Full production control for companies that make what they sell — the first of only two modules that need the Premium tier.

  • Production BOMs with version control
  • Routings, work centres and machine centres
  • Production orders with live status and costing
  • Capacity planning and finite loading
  • Subcontracting and quality management
  • Scrap, consumption and output posting
Planning

Supply Planning & MRP

The system works out what to buy and what to make — before you run short or over-order.

  • Planning worksheet balances demand against supply
  • MPS and MRP driven by orders, forecasts and lead times
  • Action messages: order, expedite, reschedule, cancel
  • Requisition worksheet for simpler planning setups
  • Safety stock and reorder points per item and location
Group 3

Projects, service & insight

Deliver the work, look after what you sold, and see the whole business clearly while you do it.

Projects & Jobs

  • Job budgets tracked against actual costs, live
  • Tasks, resources and capacity in one plan
  • Time sheets posted straight to the job
  • WIP calculation and revenue recognition
  • Bill by milestone, time and materials, or fixed price

Service Management Premium

  • Service contracts, warranties and entitlements
  • Service orders with a dispatch board
  • Service items with full repair history
  • Loaner equipment and spare-parts tracking
  • Response-time and fault reporting

Reporting & Power BI

  • Financial reporting: P&L and balance sheet built in
  • Analysis mode — pivot any list in seconds
  • Power BI dashboards for sales, stock and cash
  • Edit in Excel and publish changes back
  • Hundreds of standard reports, scheduled to inboxes

Shopify & E-commerce

  • Built-in Shopify connector, included in the licence
  • Products, prices and images sync both ways
  • Online orders and stock flow automatically
  • Shopify POS and B2B storefronts supported
  • Fewer oversells, no re-keying between systems

Human Resources

  • Employee records, contracts and qualifications
  • Absence registration and reporting
  • Confidential information held securely per employee

Deliberately basic — records, not payroll. Where you need full HR and payroll, we pair Business Central with a dedicated HR system and connect the two.

Licensing

Essentials or Premium — which tier do you need?

Two full-user tiers, one simple rule: Premium adds Manufacturing and Service Management, and nothing else. Most trading, distribution and service companies run on Essentials.

Essentials
USD 80/user/month

The complete core ERP: finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, warehouse, assembly, projects, HR records and reporting.

Premium
USD 110/user/month

Everything in Essentials, plus the only two additions Premium makes: Manufacturing and Service Management.

Team Members
USD 8/user/month

For staff who only read data, update records, approve workflows and log their time. Team Members cannot post transactions.

Microsoft list prices, per user per month, paid yearly and excluding VAT. Essentials and Premium cannot be mixed in one environment — a company runs one tier or the other. Not sure which fits? We map licences to roles before you buy, and handle implementation and licensing guidance end to end — so you never pay for seats you don’t need.

FAQs

Questions, answered

+
How much does Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central cost?
Microsoft list prices are USD 80 per user per month for Essentials and USD 110 for Premium, paid yearly and excluding VAT. Team Members licences cost USD 8 per user per month. Implementation is scoped separately — Xccel8 gives you a fixed scope and price after a discovery workshop, and maps licences to roles first so you never pay for seats you do not need.
What is the difference between Business Central Essentials and Premium?
Premium adds exactly two things to Essentials: Manufacturing and Service Management. Everything else — finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, warehousing, assembly, projects, HR records and reporting — is identical. The two tiers cannot be mixed in one environment, so a company runs one tier or the other.
Does Business Central include CRM?
Yes — a built-in relationship management module covers contacts, interaction logging, segments, campaigns and sales opportunities, and it works inside Outlook. It is not the full Dynamics 365 Sales application; companies that outgrow the built-in CRM can connect Dynamics 365 Sales through Dataverse, and Xccel8 advises on the right point to make that move. See also our CRM & Sales service.
Is Business Central cloud-based or on-premises?
Business Central is built for the cloud — it runs on Microsoft Azure as a subscription service with two major updates a year included. An on-premises deployment exists, but Copilot, the AI agents, Teams integration and the Shopify connector are cloud-only, so Xccel8 implements the online version.
Does Business Central handle UAE VAT?
Yes. As part of implementation, Xccel8 configures the VAT posting setup, tax invoices and VAT return reporting for UAE rules, so your finance team files from the same system that raised the invoices — no parallel spreadsheets at month-end.
Is e-invoicing available with Business Central in the UAE?
Yes — UAE e-invoicing is available with Business Central through Xccel8. We connect your system through our e-invoicing service so invoices are issued in the structured format the federal mandate requires, ahead of the phased deadlines — 1 January 2027 for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million and above, and 1 July 2027 for the rest.
Does Business Central support manufacturing?
Yes. The Premium tier adds full manufacturing: production bills of materials with versions, routings, work and machine centres, production orders, capacity planning and finite loading, plus subcontracting and quality management. Lighter assembly and kitting — assemble-to-order from a sales line — is already included in Essentials.
How long does a Business Central implementation take?
Typically three to six months depending on scope. A focused finance, sales and inventory rollout sits at the shorter end; manufacturing, multiple companies or complex integrations take longer. Xccel8 fixes the scope and timeline with you after the discovery and fit-gap stage, before configuration begins.
Can Business Central replace our separate accounting and inventory systems?
Yes — that is exactly what it is built for. Finance, sales, purchasing and stock share one database, so a sales order updates inventory, receivables and the general ledger at the same time. Xccel8 migrates your customers, vendors, items, open orders and balances from systems such as Tally, QuickBooks, Sage or spreadsheets as part of the implementation.
