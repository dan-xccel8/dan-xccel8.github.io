Features

What Microsoft Entra ID does for your business

Single sign-on (SSO)

One login opens every business app — no more password spreadsheets. We connect all your apps to Entra ID.

MFA & phishing-resistant logins

MFA and phishing-resistant methods stop stolen passwords cold. We roll it out company-wide without disrupting work.

Conditional access control

Access adapts to risk — location, device, user. We design policies that protect without blocking legitimate work.

User lifecycle management

Joiners get accounts automatically, leavers lose access instantly. We automate provisioning from your HR system.

Access reviews & audit reports

Regular reviews of who can access what, with audit trails for compliance. We run the review process with you.

AI-driven identity protection

Entra ID Protection flags risky sign-ins and compromised accounts automatically — we tune the detections for your environment.
