FAQ

CRM questions, answered

+
How much does Zoho CRM cost in the UAE?

Billed annually, per user per month, plus VAT: Standard AED 51, Professional AED 84, Enterprise AED 146, Ultimate AED 190. The Free edition covers up to 3 users, and monthly billing is available at a higher rate. Licences are billed by Zoho directly and cost the same through a partner — what a partner adds is the implementation, migration and training.

Will our data be hosted in the UAE?

Yes, if that's what you choose. Zoho opened UAE data centres in Dubai (primary) and Abu Dhabi (secondary) in January 2026, certified under the Dubai Electronic Security Center's CSP Security Standard — over 100 Zoho products, including CRM, are hosted there. The region is fixed when the account is created, so we confirm it before setup begins; moving an existing account between regions is handled by Zoho.

Is Zoho CRM compliant with the UAE PDPL?

Zoho provides the tooling and signs a Data Processing Addendum: consent and purpose fields, data subject access, export and deletion, and role-based access controls. Your business remains the data controller under the Federal Personal Data Protection Law (Decree-Law 45/2021), and we configure the CRM side of your duties as part of every UAE implementation. Hosting in Zoho's UAE data centres keeps the data in-country as well.

Can our sales team use WhatsApp inside Zoho CRM?

Yes. We connect WhatsApp Business to your CRM so enquiries become leads automatically, every chat is logged against the right contact, and follow-ups run from approved templates. Leads from Bayut, Property Finder, Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads can flow into the same pipelines, so no enquiry sits unseen in an app.

Does Zoho CRM work in Arabic?

Yes. Zoho CRM supports an Arabic interface with right-to-left layout, and we configure bilingual records and templates where your team needs them — so Arabic-speaking salespeople work in Arabic while management reports stay in English.

How do VAT invoicing and UAE e-invoicing work with the CRM?

Deals closed in the CRM become invoices in Zoho Books with UAE VAT applied correctly. For e-invoicing, Books connects to the federal system through a Ministry of Finance-accredited ASP partner — large businesses go live on 1 January 2027, with smaller businesses following in 2027. We set up the connection and test it with you, so compliance is in place before your deadline.

Can you migrate us from Salesforce, HubSpot or spreadsheets?

Yes. We cleanse your data, map your fields, rebuild your pipelines and automations in Zoho CRM and import your history, validating everything with your team before go-live. A standard sales setup typically takes 1–3 weeks; mid-market implementations with integrations and migration usually take 4–8 weeks — confirmed after the discovery workshop, before you commit.

Zoho CRM vs CRM Plus — which one fits us?

Zoho CRM alone suits most sales teams. CRM Plus bundles CRM with support, marketing and analytics apps — worth it when you want one platform across departments. We advise based on what you actually need, not the bigger bundle.
