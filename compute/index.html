Does XCCEL8 sell servers and storage in UAE?

Yes. Yes. We implement backup targets, disaster recovery and ransomware-resilient architectures with solutions from Veeam, ExaGrid, Acronis and others.

It depends on workload, compliance and cost. Our free consultation assesses your needs and recommends the right mix of cloud (Azure, AWS) and on-prem infrastructure.
