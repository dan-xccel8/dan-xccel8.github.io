Capabilities

Features

Payroll (UAE-localized)

  • Salary structures with basic, allowances and deductions
  • WPS (Wage Protection System) salary files for UAE bank processing
  • End-of-service / gratuity accrual as per UAE labour law
  • Payslips issued to employees digitally
  • Loans and advances tracked against payroll
  • Payroll reports for finance and auditing

Employee records

  • Central employee database — profiles, documents, visas and contracts in one place
  • Organisation chart and reporting lines
  • Document expiry alerts — visas, passports, labour cards
  • Onboarding checklists for new joiners, exit workflows for leavers

Attendance

  • Biometric and access-device integration for clock in/out
  • Shift and roster management, including rotating shifts
  • Overtime calculation per your company policy
  • Attendance regularisation workflows for missed punches
  • Mobile check-in for field and site staff

Leave

  • Leave policies configured to your rules and UAE requirements
  • Annual, sick, maternity/paternity and unpaid leave types
  • Approval workflows — requests routed to the right manager
  • Live leave balances for employees and HR
  • UAE public holidays built into the calendar

Performance

  • Appraisal cycles with goal setting and reviews
  • KPI tracking per role or department

Employee self-service

  • Staff apply for leave, view payslips and update details themselves
  • Mobile app for attendance, approvals and requests on the go
  • Manager dashboards — team attendance, pending approvals, headcount

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is WPS and do we need it?

The Wage Protection System is how UAE employers pay staff through the banking system. We generate compliant WPS salary files from your payroll every cycle.

Does it calculate gratuity?

Yes — end-of-service accrual runs as per UAE labour law, visible per employee.

Can it connect to our biometric attendance devices?

Yes — attendance punches flow from your devices into payroll-relevant records.

Do employees get self-service?

Yes — leave applications, payslips and attendance on web and mobile.
