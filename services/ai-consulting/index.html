How it works

One journey, five phases.
You approve every step.

A fixed, transparent process from first assessment to confident daily use — with your involvement and approval crucial at every stage.

1

Assessment 1–2 weeks

We map your processes, review your systems and data, and check what’s ready for AI and what isn’t. You get a ranked roadmap: which opportunities matter most, what each one takes, and whether your current systems can carry AI or need a change.

2

Design Solution 2–3 weeks

We design a solution with your priorities in mind — integrated with your real systems, building ones you lack, test in a sandbox, measured against agreed targets. Your involvement and approval is crucial at this stage.

3

Implementation 3–6 weeks

The design expands into production: process flows, integrations with your existing software, building new ones, training the AI Agents, guardrails for security and approvals. This is the core build.

4

Training 1–2 weeks

Your team learns to work with the new tools — not as a one-off session, but as part of how they do their jobs. We take training seriously.

5

Hyper Support 6 weeks

We stay with you while the team adapts: monitoring, tuning, and refining based on real daily use.

AMC — optional but recommended. This includes continuous tuning which will be required, along with adopting new future-AI innovations.
