FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes — we build multi-platform apps for Android and iOS. One product is designed properly first, then built for both platforms so your customers and team get the same experience wherever they are.

Is it one build or two separate apps?

For most projects it is one build: cross-platform technologies such as React Native or Flutter let one codebase serve both Android and iOS. Where an app needs deep device features or maximum performance, we recommend fully native Swift and Kotlin builds instead.

Do you handle App Store and Google Play publishing?

Yes — we handle App Store and Google Play submission, including store listings, screenshots and responding to review feedback, and we manage updates and new releases after launch.

Can the app integrate with our existing systems?

Yes — we integrate apps with Zoho and Microsoft systems, payment gateways and third-party APIs, so data flows between the app and the systems your business already runs on.

Do you maintain and update the app after launch?

Yes — ongoing care covers monitoring, fixes, operating-system compatibility updates and planned feature releases, so the app keeps working as phones and platforms change.
