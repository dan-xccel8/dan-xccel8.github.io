FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
What does ELV mean?

ELV stands for extra-low voltage — the building systems that run on low-voltage cabling rather than mains power: CCTV, access control, fire alarm and detection, structured cabling, public address, gate barriers with ANPR and building automation (BMS).

Which ELV systems does a typical UAE building need?

Most commercial buildings need four core systems — CCTV, access control, a fire alarm system and structured cabling — with AV, public address and building automation added by building type, and gate barriers with ANPR wherever there is parking or a controlled entrance. Villas usually start with CCTV, a video intercom and structured cabling for Wi-Fi. We scope the exact set on a free site survey.

Do you handle SIRA compliance for CCTV?

Yes. CCTV in commercial premises in Dubai must meet SIRA requirements. We design your system to those standards, and installation is carried out through our SIRA-certified partners, so your premises pass inspection.

Can you add ELV systems to an existing building?

Yes. Most of our ELV work is retrofits into occupied offices, buildings and villas. PoE and wireless options reduce new cabling, and we phase the work around your operations so the building keeps running.

Can the different ELV systems work together?

Yes, where the platforms support it — an access-control event can call up the matching camera view, an ANPR read can open a barrier for a known vehicle, and alarms can surface in the building management system. We decide the integrations at design stage so the systems are specified to work as one.

Do you maintain ELV systems after installation?

Yes. Our AMC cover includes scheduled testing — including fire alarm tests — system health checks, firmware updates and callouts, so your systems stay compliant and working year after year.
