FAQ

Frequently asked questions

We already use Teams/Zoom — what do you add?

The room itself: certified video bars, mics and displays that make Teams/Zoom meetings actually work in a physical room, plus one-touch join so nobody fiddles with cables.

Can you fit out a single huddle room, or only full offices?

Either. Single rooms and floor-wide rollouts both start with a survey.

Do you handle the phone lines (Etisalat/du)?

We design the system and configure SIP/VoIP connectivity alongside your provider's lines.

Do you maintain the rooms after installation?

Yes — support and AMC contracts cover moves, changes and break-fix.
