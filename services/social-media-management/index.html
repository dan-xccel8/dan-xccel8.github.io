FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Google Business Profile and WhatsApp. Most businesses do not need all nine — we recommend the mix based on where your customers actually spend their time, and manage those properly rather than spreading thinly across everything.

Do we approve content before it goes out?

Yes. Every month's content is planned in a content calendar and shared with you for approval before anything is scheduled or published. Your tone of voice is agreed at the start, and nothing posts that you have not seen.

Who creates the content — do we need to supply photos and videos?

We plan, design and write the content for you — posts, captions and short-form video built around your products and services. Material you share with us, such as photos from your premises or your team, is worked into the calendar; you do not need a content team of your own.

Does social media management include advertising?

No. Management covers your organic presence — content, publishing and community. Paid campaigns are a separate service (Paid Ad Management), and ad spend is always paid by you directly to the platform. The two work best together: ads bring people to profiles that post every day.

What reporting do we get?

A monthly report covering what was published, how each platform performed in reach and engagement, how your audience grew, and the enquiries and WhatsApp conversations your content produced — along with the plan for the month ahead.
