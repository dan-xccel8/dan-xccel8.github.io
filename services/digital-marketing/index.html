FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
What does the full stack include?

Everything a business needs to be seen and chosen: digital marketing (SEO, GEO and AEO optimisation, websites, social media, email and WhatsApp drip campaigns, webinars and paid ads), influencer marketing, and traditional marketing — outdoor, print, radio, TV, cinema, transit, events and branded materials — planned as one stack and reported in enquiries.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings or mentions in AI answers?

No — and be cautious of anyone who does. Rankings cannot be guaranteed, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini do not sell promotion inside their answers. What we do is the optimisation that earns visibility: content, structure, authority and citations, with transparent reporting on what it produces.

How much does influencer marketing cost?

It is proportionate to the influencer's number of relevant followers or subscribers and the frequency of posts and promotions during the collaboration. We identify influencers based on your budget, from micro and nano creators to major names.

How do paid campaigns work?

You set the budget; we plan, run and optimise the campaigns within it — lead-generation focused or PR and branding focused — across Google, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, Snapchat and TikTok Ads, with every enquiry tracked back to its campaign.

Can you handle traditional marketing and events, or only digital?

The full stack. Alongside digital and influencer marketing we run outdoor, print, radio, TV, cinema and transit advertising, full end-to-end events from conferences to private celebrations, and design and production of branded business materials.

Do you create content in Arabic as well?

Yes. Campaigns run in English and Arabic, so you reach the whole UAE audience across search, social and traditional media.
