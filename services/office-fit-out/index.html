New Office IT & ELV Fit-Out

One contract takes your new office from empty floor to move-in day — structured cabling, Wi-Fi, CCTV, access control, meeting rooms & AV, server room and telephony, phased to your fit-out programme in Dubai and across the UAE.

Capabilities

Features

Structured cabling & data points

  • Cat6A data points to every desk, meeting room and device position on the plan
  • Fibre backbone between floors and comms rooms where the layout needs it
  • First fix during fit-out, while ceilings and partitions are still open
  • Every point Fluke-tested, labelled and documented — see our structured cabling service

Wi-Fi, switching & network core

  • Wi-Fi designed from the floor plan — coverage and capacity planned before a single access point goes up
  • Managed switching, routing and firewall configured as one network
  • Guest, staff and IoT traffic segmented from day one
  • Internet handover coordinated so the network is live before your team arrives

CCTV surveillance

  • Camera coverage for entrances, work areas, stores and parking
  • Designed to SIRA requirements for Dubai commercial premises, installed through our SIRA-certified partners
  • Recording, retention and remote viewing set up and handed over

Access control & biometrics

  • Fingerprint, face-recognition and card access for doors, server rooms and stores
  • Time attendance that feeds payroll and WPS reporting
  • Visitor and contractor access designed into the plan, not bolted on later

Meeting rooms & AV

  • Video bars, displays and one-touch join for huddle rooms, boardrooms and training spaces
  • Room booking panels and digital signage where the design calls for them
  • Telephony — IP phones, softphones and connectivity — set up alongside the rooms

Server room & power

  • Rack build, patching and labelling in your comms or server room
  • UPS and rack power sized to the actual load
  • Environmental monitoring for temperature and leaks from day one

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
When should IT be involved in an office fit-out?

From the start. Cabling and containment go in before ceilings and partitions close, so involving us at design stage is what makes data points, Wi-Fi and cameras land in the right places without rework.

Can one contract cover cabling, Wi-Fi, CCTV and meeting rooms?

Yes — that is the point of this service: one design, one programme and one accountable contractor for the full IT and ELV scope, instead of separate vendors blaming each other when systems don't work together.

Do you coordinate with our fit-out contractor?

Yes — we work to your fit-out programme, join site coordination, and phase first-fix and second-fix work around the other trades so nothing holds up the handover.

Our move-in date is fixed — can you work backwards from it?

Yes — we plan backwards from move-in day, sequence the critical path first (cabling, network core, internet handover) and test everything before your team arrives.

Do you handle refits of existing offices too?

Yes — phased refits of occupied offices, including re-cabling, network upgrades and meeting-room refreshes, scheduled to keep the business running while the work happens.
