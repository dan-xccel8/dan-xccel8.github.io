Three disciplines, one goal

Be found, be recommended, be the answer

Customers now search three ways — they Google it, they ask an AI tool, and they take the direct answer they are given. Each way needs its own optimisation.

SEO — Search Engine Optimisation

Technical health, on-page optimisation and local presence — site structure, speed, metadata, internal linking, Google Business Profile and Maps — so Google can crawl, understand and rank the pages your customers are searching for.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation

Being mentioned and recommended when customers ask AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini who to use. That visibility is earned — through clear content, strong structure, genuine authority and citations across the web — never bought as placement inside an answer.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation

Answer-first content built to be picked up as the answer: question-form headings, direct answers up front, FAQ sections and schema markup, so search engines and AI tools can lift your answer word for word.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO gets your pages found and ranked in search results like Google. GEO gets your business mentioned and recommended inside AI answers from tools like ChatGPT and Gemini. AEO structures your content — answer-first writing, question-form headings and FAQ schema — so search engines and AI tools can pick your answer up directly. The three work together: strong SEO foundations feed both GEO and AEO.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI mentions?

No — and be cautious of anyone who does. Rankings cannot be guaranteed, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini do not sell promotion inside their answers. What we do is the optimisation that earns visibility: content, structure, authority and citations, with transparent reporting on what it produces.

How long before optimisation shows results?

Technical and on-page fixes can improve how your site is indexed within weeks, but rankings, authority and AI mentions build over months as content and citations accumulate. We report progress monthly — rankings, traffic, enquiries and AI visibility — so you can see the trend rather than take it on faith.

What reporting do we get?

A monthly report covering rankings, organic traffic, enquiries generated, backlinks and citations gained, and checks on how AI tools describe and recommend businesses in your category — plus the work done and what is planned next.

Do we need a new website, or can you optimise the one we have?

Usually the one you have. The audit tells us what is holding the site back; most sites need technical fixes, better structure and stronger content rather than a rebuild. If a rebuild genuinely is the faster route, we will show you why before recommending it.
