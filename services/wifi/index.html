Capabilities

Features

Site survey & design

  • On-site wireless survey — signal mapping, interference and dead-zone analysis
  • Access-point placement planned per floor plan and user density
  • Capacity planning — designed for your device count, not just floor area
  • Heatmap reports showing predicted coverage before we install

Access points

  • Indoor access points for offices, retail and hospitality
  • Outdoor and weatherproof units for courtyards, parking and compounds
  • High-density models for auditoriums, majlis areas and event spaces
  • Latest Wi-Fi standards matched to your device mix

Mesh networks

  • Wireless mesh where cabling to every point isn't practical
  • Self-healing links — traffic reroutes if a node drops
  • Ideal for villas, warehouses and retrofit buildings

Campus & full-site coverage

  • Multi-building coverage with unified management
  • Seamless roaming — calls and sessions stay alive as users move between access points
  • Outdoor coverage tied into the same network as indoors

Guest Wi-Fi

  • Separate guest network isolated from your business traffic
  • Captive portal with your branding and terms acceptance
  • Voucher or timed access for visitors and events

Security

  • WPA3 encryption and strong authentication
  • Network segmentation — staff, guests, IoT and cameras on separate lanes
  • Rogue access-point detection

Management & monitoring

  • Centralised cloud or on-premise controller — one dashboard for all sites
  • Live client, traffic and health monitoring
  • Alerts for offline access points and unusual usage
  • Firmware and policy updates handled under AMC
