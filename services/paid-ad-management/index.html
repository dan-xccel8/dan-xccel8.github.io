FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
Who controls the ad budget?

You do. You set the budget; we plan, launch and optimise the campaigns within it, watch spend daily and move it towards what produces enquiries. Nothing runs beyond the budget you have approved.

Should our campaigns focus on lead generation or branding?

Most businesses need lead generation first — campaigns built and measured on enquiries. Branding and PR-focused campaigns build reach and recognition over time and work best alongside lead generation, not instead of it. We will recommend the split based on your market and goals.

What reporting do we get?

Reporting that follows the money: spend by campaign and platform, and the calls, forms and WhatsApp enquiries each one produced — tracked back to the individual campaign, so you can see exactly what your budget bought.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, X and WhatsApp — plus high-relevance listing platforms. We recommend the mix based on where your buyers actually are, not on running everywhere at once.

Do we need landing pages, or can ads point to our homepage?

Dedicated landing pages, in almost every case. A page built for one campaign and one offer — with its own tracking — converts far better than a homepage, and it is what lets us A/B test and prove what is working.
