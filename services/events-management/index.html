FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
What types of events do you manage?

Business conferences and seminars, product launches, corporate celebrations, exhibitions and expo presence, weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, bachelor parties and private celebrations — from small private events to large corporate-style occasions.

What does end-to-end handling actually include?

Venue, staging and AV, catering coordination, branding and materials, guest and registration management, promotion before the event, and photography and aftermovie coordination — planned and run by one team, with our network of experienced event personnel in the region on the day.

Can you promote our event as well as run it?

Yes. Promotion before the event — social, email and WhatsApp campaigns, registration pages and reminder sequences — is part of the service for corporate and public events, and photography and aftermovie coordination keeps the event visible afterwards too.

Do you handle small private events, or only large corporate ones?

Both. The service covers small private celebrations through to large corporate-style occasions; the planning and production discipline is the same, scaled to the size of the event.

How do we start, and what do you need from us?

A short brief: the occasion, the date, the approximate guest count and the kind of experience you want. From there we propose venue and format options and a full plan — you approve the plan and the budget, and we handle everything else.
