    Good to know

    Why is this an estimate, not a quote?

    Because honest pricing depends on the site. Cable distances, ceiling types, wall construction, existing infrastructure that can be reused, and the equipment brands you choose all move the final number — anyone who quotes exactly without looking is guessing with your budget. The estimator gives you a realistic range for planning; the free survey turns it into an exact, itemised quote. The systems themselves are covered in depth on our CCTV & IP cameras, biometrics & access control, conference room, structured cabling and office fit-out pages.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    +
    How accurate is the price estimator?

    It gives an indicative range based on typical UAE projects of the size you describe — enough to budget and compare options. It is not a quotation: exact pricing depends on your site, cable distances, ceiling and wall construction, and the equipment brands selected, which is why your final quote is confirmed after a free site survey.

    Is the site survey really free?

    Yes. Send us your estimate through the form on this page and we will arrange a site survey at no charge, then confirm your exact, itemised quote against what we find on site.

    What does the indicative range include?

    The range covers the equipment, and the installation, cabling and first-year maintenance options you tick, for the quantities you enter. Your exact quote is itemised line by line after the survey, so you can see equipment, installation and cabling separately and adjust the scope before you commit.

    Which systems can I estimate?

    Five: CCTV, access control, video conferencing, structured cabling and complete office IT & ELV fit-out. If your project mixes several systems — a new office usually does — run the office fit-out estimate for the combined picture, or send us the details and we will scope it in one quote.

    How quickly will I get my exact quote?

    We respond within one business day to arrange the survey. After the survey you receive an itemised quotation — most straightforward projects are quoted within a few working days of the visit.

    Can I just send you my estimate?

    Yes — that is what the form below is for. Your estimate summary is attached to the enquiry automatically, so when we call you back we are already talking about your project, not starting from zero.
