The problem

Your customers are on WhatsApp — is your business?

Most UAE customers would rather message a business on WhatsApp than call or email. But when that number lives on one person's phone, chats get missed and nothing is recorded. The WhatsApp Business API fixes that.

WhatsApp Business — free

  • Runs on one phone — a single user at a time
  • Manual replies and quick replies only
  • Broadcasts capped at 256 contacts, and only to people who have saved your number
  • Basic product catalogue and business profile
  • No connection to your CRM — chats stay on the phone
  • Right for a solo operator with a handful of chats a day

WhatsApp Business API — with XCCEL8

  • One number, many staff — a shared team inbox
  • Chatbots and automated replies answer routine questions day and night
  • Broadcasts at scale using pre-approved message templates
  • Order, delivery and payment notifications sent automatically
  • Every conversation logged against the customer in your CRM
  • Built for teams that sell and support on WhatsApp every day

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business App and the WhatsApp Business API?

The free app runs on one phone for one person — fine for a solo operator. The API is the team version: many staff share one business number in a single inbox, with chatbots, broadcast campaigns, automated notifications and a full record of every conversation in your CRM.

Do we own our WhatsApp number?

Yes. The number is registered to your business, and your customer data stays yours. If you already use a number on the WhatsApp Business App, we can usually migrate that same number to the API so customers keep messaging the number they know.

How do broadcast message approvals work?

Marketing and notification messages sent outside an open chat use templates that Meta approves in advance. We draft your templates — offers, reminders, order updates — and submit them as part of your setup, so your campaigns are ready to send from day one.

How much does the WhatsApp Business API cost?

Three parts: a subscription for the platform you choose, Meta's per-conversation charges, and our one-time setup and integration. After a short assessment we quote the whole thing as one clear number — no obligation.

Can our whole team really use one WhatsApp number?

Yes — that is the main reason businesses move to the API. Every agent works from the same number in a shared inbox: chats can be assigned, internal notes added, and managers can see response times and open conversations.

Can WhatsApp connect to our CRM and website?

Yes. We connect your WhatsApp number to Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Odoo and other systems, and add chat widgets and click-to-chat links to your website — so enquiries land in your CRM with the full conversation attached.
