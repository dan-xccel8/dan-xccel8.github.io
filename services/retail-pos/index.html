A good POS disappears into the working day — fast billing at the counter, kitchen tickets firing automatically, stock updating itself across branches. We supply and set up the full stack: software plus terminals, scanners and printers, configured for your business type.

Software

Software features

Retail stores

  • Fast barcode-driven billing with discounts, promotions and loyalty
  • Returns, exchanges and store credit handling
  • Centralised pricing and promotions across branches
  • Cashier shifts, user roles and permissions
  • Daily sales, product and cashier reports

Restaurants & cafes

  • KOT — kitchen order tickets fired automatically to the kitchen
  • Table management with floor plans, table status and transfers
  • Modifiers, combos, split bills and merged tables
  • Takeaway and delivery modes with separate order flows
  • Kitchen printers or kitchen display routing

Supermarkets & groceries

  • High-volume barcode checkout built for speed at the till
  • Weighing-scale integration for fresh produce
  • Batch and expiry tracking on relevant items
  • Supplier purchases and goods-received recording

Across all of it

  • UAE VAT-ready tax calculation and invoice formats
  • Works offline — billing continues if the internet drops, syncs when it returns
  • Real-time stock deduction as items sell
  • Multi-branch dashboard — sales, stock and staff in one view
  • Links to accounting and inventory so the back office stays in sync

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can one system run multiple branches?

Yes — menus, prices, promotions and reports are managed centrally, with per-branch views and stock.

Does it handle restaurant needs like KOT and table management?

Yes — kitchen tickets, table plans, modifiers, split bills and takeaway/delivery modes.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Billing continues offline and syncs automatically when the connection returns.

What hardware will I need?

Depends on your counters and volume — typically touchscreen terminals, scanners, receipt printers and cash drawers. We specify and supply the lot.
