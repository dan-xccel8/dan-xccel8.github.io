FAQ

Frequently asked questions

+
Do we approve the design before you build the website?

Yes — every website is designed professionally in Figma first, and you review and approve the design before any code is built. Changes happen at design stage, where they are quick and clear.

Which technologies do you build websites with?

We work with Astro, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, React and Next.js, WordPress, and Shopify or WooCommerce for e-commerce. The technology is chosen per project based on what the site needs to do and who will maintain it.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

Yes — we build e-commerce websites with product catalogues, secure checkout, payment gateways and shipping and tax set up for UAE and international orders, connected to your inventory and accounting where needed.

Can our website connect to Zoho or Microsoft systems?

Yes — we integrate Zoho and Microsoft systems into your website, including CRM enquiry capture, bookings, payments, customer portals and data sync, so enquiries and orders flow straight into the systems your team already uses.

Do you handle hosting, domain, SSL and ongoing maintenance?

Yes — we set up hosting, domain and SSL properly at launch, and ongoing care covers updates, backups, security checks and small changes, so the site stays fast, secure and current.
