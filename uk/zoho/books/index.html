Features

What Zoho Books does for your business

Invoicing & payment links

Invoices carry a pay-now link: Stripe, PayPal and GoCardless Direct Debit connected, links generated and shared from inside Books. Automatic reminders keep late payers moving.

VAT under Making Tax Digital

Zoho Books is HMRC-recognised MTD software: digital VAT records kept properly and returns filed to HMRC from inside Books. Flat Rate and Cash Accounting schemes configured to match your registration.

Corporation Tax & CIS

File your CT600 corporation tax return to HMRC and Companies House from Books, and run CIS deductions and returns if you work in construction. Year-end stops being a separate project.

E-invoicing: ready for 2029

The UK has confirmed mandatory e-invoicing for all VAT invoices from April 2029, over the Peppol network. Books already speaks Peppol: we set your invoicing up clean now, so 2029 is a switch, not a scramble.

Payroll, the UK way

Zoho Payroll's UK edition calculates PAYE and National Insurance, submits RTI (FPS/EPS) to HMRC, runs auto-enrolment pensions and statutory pay — then posts every pay run's journals into Books automatically.

Pounds at home, any currency abroad

Your books run in GBP; multi-currency transactions convert at live rates. Overseas sales get the right VAT treatment — zero-rating and reverse charge set up once, applied every time.

One design on every document

Custom templates carry one unified design across quotations, sales orders, invoices, retainers, credit notes, debit notes, purchase orders, vendor credits and payment receipts — your brand, identical on every document a customer or supplier sees.

Roles, approvals & access control

Custom roles decide who sees and does what, down to the module. Multi-stage approvals hold invoices, bills and purchase orders until the right people have signed off — nothing leaves unchecked.

Reports: standard & custom

50+ standard reports — profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT summaries — plus custom reports built around your questions, scheduled to land in the right inboxes automatically.

Inventory, your way

Start with Books' built-in inventory: stock levels, reorder alerts, price lists. Need warehouses, batches and serial numbers? We configure Zoho Inventory — or Books Elite — and connect the two properly.

Retail-ready with Zoho POS

Zoho POS is available in the UK: tills bill in pounds, stock updates in real time and the day's sales land in Books. Already running a POS? We integrate it professionally instead.

Honest pricing in pounds

Zoho bills licences directly in GBP at the UK list price — Free plan under £35,000 revenue; Standard £10, Professional £20, Premium £25 per organisation/month billed annually (+VAT). No markup; we charge for implementation, migration and training.

FAQ

Books questions, answered

+
Is Zoho Books recognised by HMRC for Making Tax Digital?

Yes. Zoho Books is HMRC-recognised software for MTD for VAT — mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses since April 2022 — and for MTD for Income Tax, which sole traders and landlords with income over £50,000 follow from April 2026. We complete your HMRC authorisation during setup and file a test return with you before handover.

When does UK e-invoicing become mandatory, and is Books ready?

From April 2029, all VAT invoices in B2B and B2G transactions must be issued as e-invoices; the government confirmed the mandate at Autumn Budget 2025, with Peppol as the core network. Zoho Books already exchanges invoices over Peppol — public-sector suppliers use it today — so a clean setup now makes 2029 a formality.

What does Zoho Books cost in the UK?

Zoho bills licences directly in GBP, plus VAT: Free plan for revenue under £35,000; Standard £10, Professional £20, Premium £25 per organisation/month billed annually. We recommend the plan that fits your actual usage — never seats or modules you don't need.

Can you migrate us from Xero, QuickBooks or Sage?

Yes. We migrate your chart of accounts, customers, suppliers, opening balances and transaction history, run the old system in parallel until the numbers agree, then switch over. A standard migration and implementation typically takes 1–3 weeks; complex or multi-entity setups 4–8 weeks.

Does Zoho Books handle UK payroll?

Through Zoho Payroll's UK edition, connected to Books: PAYE and National Insurance calculated, RTI submissions (FPS/EPS) sent to HMRC, workplace pensions and auto-enrolment managed, statutory pay and student loans applied — and every pay run's journals posted into Books automatically. P45, P60 and P11D forms are produced in the system.

Can Books file our corporation tax and CIS returns?

Yes. Books files your CT600 corporation tax return to HMRC and your final accounts to Companies House, and handles CIS deductions and returns for construction. We configure both during implementation, matched to how your business is registered.

Where is our data hosted?

UK accounts are created in Zoho's European data centres, in Amsterdam and Dublin, and the region is fixed at signup. We configure roles, access controls and audit trails so your UK GDPR housekeeping is in order from day one.

Zoho Books vs Xero — what do we gain by switching?

One connected suite instead of a patchwork: CRM, payroll, inventory and POS from the same vendor on one login, MTD filing built in rather than bolted on, and per-organisation pricing that stays flat as your team grows. We migrate your Xero history across cleanly, so nothing is lost in the move.
